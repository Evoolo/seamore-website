# Sea More Expeditions — brand guide

![Sea More Expeditions modernized logo](assets/images/logo-modern.png)

## Brand at a glance

**Sea More Expeditions** creates wildlife-focused diving, snorkeling, and nature journeys. It chooses destinations for the chance to encounter particular animals in their natural habitat, then builds the travel experience around those encounters. Sharks, crocodiles, whale sharks, orcas, manta rays, and other marine life are recurring subjects. The brand's founding story moves from fear of sharks toward fascination, understanding, and respect. [Source: History](CONTENT.md#article-135) · [Source: Why us](CONTENT.md#article-159)

**Purpose:** Help people experience remarkable wildlife firsthand, so they can better understand and respect it. [Source: History](CONTENT.md#article-135)

**Positioning statement:** For curious travelers and divers who want meaningful wildlife encounters, Sea More Expeditions offers carefully planned, personally supported journeys to places selected for the animals people hope to see. [Source: Why us](CONTENT.md#article-159)

**Short description:** Wildlife expeditions shaped by experience, curiosity, and personal attention.

## Core values

These values are **editorial interpretations** of the site content, not a formally published values list.

1. **Curiosity and respect for wildlife.** Encourage people to learn about animals through firsthand experience. Use wonder and accurate observation instead of fear-based language. [Source](CONTENT.md#article-135)
2. **Experience-led planning.** Select locations for the wildlife encounter sought, and work with experienced local professionals. Explain the thinking behind each itinerary. [Source](CONTENT.md#article-159)
3. **Care for the traveler.** Provide personal help before and during guided trips, especially with diving, photography, and local practicalities. [Source](CONTENT.md#article-159)
4. **Choice and flexibility.** Adapt journeys to individual interests, travel styles, and desired level of support. [Source: Custom tours](CONTENT.md#article-132) · [Source: Exclusive travel](CONTENT.md#article-133)
5. **Shared adventure.** Make group travel welcoming and relaxed, with space to meet people who share a passion for the natural world. [Source](CONTENT.md#article-161)

## Audience

- Divers and snorkelers seeking particular marine wildlife encounters.
- Nature and wildlife photographers who value access, guidance, and time in the field.
- Curious travelers who want a guided group journey, a custom itinerary, or a private trip.

The site also speaks to people who feel uncertain about animals such as sharks and want to understand them through experience. [Source: History](CONTENT.md#article-135)

## Voice and messaging

**Sound:** Knowledgeable, welcoming, curious, and direct. Pair the excitement of a rare encounter with practical detail about how a trip works.

**Lead with:** The animal or natural phenomenon, why the location was chosen, what guests will do, the level of guidance, and what can vary with conditions.

**Avoid:** Guaranteed sightings, claims that an encounter is risk-free, sensational descriptions of predators, or outdated prices presented as current. The archived site says location choice improves the chance of encounters; it does not make them certain. [Source](CONTENT.md#article-159)

**Example headline:** “Get closer to the wildlife you came to see.”

**Example introduction:** “We plan journeys around the animals that fascinate you. Travel with experienced guides to carefully chosen locations, learn as you go, and make the expedition your own.”
